E1 · Twelve-country 3×2 city-council survey experiment

Class 1 · Primary experiment: respondents in 12 countries were randomized among six vignette cells crossing council composition (three levels) with policy issue (two levels), followed by formally analyzed legitimacy outcomes. Country, issue-area, subgroup, and robustness analyses reuse this assignment and are not separate experiments.
