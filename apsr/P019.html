E1 · Survey-embedded 4×2 factorial hosting-appeals experiment

Class 1 · This is the article's only manipulated randomized study and the experiment used for its policy-facing causal claim. Four appeal states were crossed with two ethnicity primes in the September 2019 household survey. Immediate, two-week, and 10-month outcomes reuse the original assignment and therefore belong to this one experiment.
