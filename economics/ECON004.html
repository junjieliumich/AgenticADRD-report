EXP1 · 2008 Ghana secondary-school scholarship-offer lottery

Class 1 · Primary experiment: 2,064 eligible individuals were randomly assigned to scholarship offer versus no scholarship; all 2008–2023 follow-ups and the assignment-instrumented 2SLS use this original randomization.

Target population

Qualified but financially constrained youth in selected rural Ghanaian districts who had not enrolled in senior high school.
qualified but financially constrained students in rural GhanaPDF page 34 · Conclusion

Eligibility criteria

Passed BECE and received CSSPS placement into one of 177 public day SHSs; attended JHS in the same district; had not enrolled in any SHS by October 2008 and still had not enrolled by the January 2009 callback. The frame covered 54 rural districts in five regions; some 2007 female BECE takers were added to reach 50% female.

Recruitment procedure

Schools identified admitted non-enrollees; researchers checked JHS records, visited homes, and sampled until the budget target of about 2,250 was reached. The total eligible population and a complete sampling fraction are unknown.
