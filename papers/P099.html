Study overview

Research question

Whether Ukrainians trade political autonomy and territorial integrity against civilian deaths, military deaths, and nuclear risk, or instead take a categorical stance toward self-defense.

Design

One face-to-face conjoint survey experiment with 1,160 Ukrainians, fielded July 16–24, 2022. Respondents evaluated four pairs (eight total) of military-political strategies.

Principal result

The article reports that respondents strongly preferred full political autonomy and territorial integrity; those concerns outweighed the three experimentally varied war-cost attributes.

Source and extraction scope

The sole study source was the complete 19-page P099.pdf, including its main text, footnotes, tables, figures, captions, references, and the final list of online supporting-information appendices. Those appendices are named but are not embedded in the supplied PDF, so appendix-only details were not imported or reconstructed.

Experiment summaries

E1 · Face-to-face conjoint survey experiment

PDF-supported label: “face-to-face conjoint survey experiment” (Research Design, printed p. 1464) and “Survey Experiment Design” (subsection heading).

Unit and task: 1,160 Ukrainian respondents; four paired comparisons, with a rating for each profile followed by a forced choice. Profiles independently varied five attributes across three levels.

Scope: Ukrainians in regions judged safe for face-to-face interviews, excluding actively contested regions and people displaced since February 24, 2022.

Unresolved gaps and limitations

  • The supplied PDF states that detailed specifications, summary statistics, robustness material, and the pre-analysis plan are in online Supporting Information, but those appendices are not contained in this PDF. They were not accessed.
  • The article does not report a general item-level missing-data procedure in the supplied PDF, nor a treatment-fidelity measure, nor explicit assumptions or checks about respondent communication, contamination, or interference.
  • No finding is coded Pending: the readable PDF was sufficient to describe what it directly reports and to identify the bounded absence assessments.
  • “Not reported” means only that the item was not located in the reviewed PDF; it does not imply the procedure did not occur.

Extraction-method note

The PDF was read as a complete document. Claims were split into atomic component-level records, assigned to the experiment and requested feature group, then given deterministic IDs after the inventory was complete. Explicit records retain qualifications, units, denominators, confidence intervals, and scope. Counts on this page are derived at runtime from the embedded finding records. Existing JSON, HTML, spreadsheets, notes, prior reports, and the web were not used.
