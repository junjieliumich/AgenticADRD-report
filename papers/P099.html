Study overview

The study asks whether Ukrainians trade the costs of armed self-defense against territorial integrity and political autonomy. It reports a face-to-face conjoint survey experiment with 1,160 Ukrainians in July 2022. Respondents evaluated paired war strategies varying territorial concessions, civilian and military fatalities, nuclear risk, and political outcome.

Experiment summary

E1 · Conjoint survey experiment

Between July 16 and 24, 2022, 1,160 respondents each rated and chose between four pairs of military-political strategies. Profiles varied five three-level attributes with equal level probabilities. The paper estimates AMCEs, conditional AMCEs, marginal means, heterogeneity, and exploratory nested marginal means.

Experiment label follows the paper’s wording; sequential ID assigned in source order.

Extraction-method note

The PDF was read page by page in text and rendered form. Atomic claims were assigned after the experiment inventory was complete, then numbered deterministically within experiment–feature cells. Counts below and above are calculated from the embedded finding records at load time.
