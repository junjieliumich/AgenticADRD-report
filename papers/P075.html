Preventing Rebel Resurgence after Civil War: A Field Experiment in Security and Justice Provision in Rural Colombia

PDF-grounded comparison of the community-randomized ComunPaz evaluation and its embedded endorsement and list experiments.

Study overview

The study evaluates ComunPaz, a four-module program designed to improve information, trust, and coordination among police officers, Police Inspectors, Juntas de Acción Comunal (JACs), and citizens in rural Colombian communities formerly dominated by FARC.

The main experiment randomized treatment at the community level across 149 communities. An endline roughly seven months after implementation combined resident, leader, police, and Police Inspector surveys with petition and WhatsApp behavioral measures. The resident survey also contained an endorsement experiment and a list experiment.

Evidence-coverage summary

Experiment summaries

Experiment 1

ComunPaz program field experiment

Community-level assignment to a four-module coordination program versus control. Primary evaluation outcomes cover disputes, reliance on authorities, knowledge, perceptions, and coordination.

Experiment 2

endorsement experiment

Resident-level randomized endorsement of three policies by JACs, police, armed groups, or no endorser, measuring policy approval and how ComunPaz changed endorsement effects.

Experiment 3

list experiment

An indirect resident-survey experiment measuring reliance on armed groups. The contained PDF reports only a high-level result; design and model details reside in an uncontained appendix.

Unresolved gaps and limitations

  • The article’s referenced appendices and supplementary material are not embedded in P075.pdf. Their results and procedures were not accessed or reconstructed.
  • The PDF gives only a high-level description of the list experiment; its items, allocation procedure, denominator, estimator, and missing-data handling remain unreported in the reviewed material.
  • Multiple-comparison corrections and balance tests are referenced to uncontained appendices and cannot be verified from the sole permitted source.
  • No finding is marked Pending: the contained pages were readable, and unresolved omissions are coded as “Not reported in reviewed materials,” without implying the procedures did not occur.

Extraction-method note

  • Read all 20 pages and checked rendered layouts for tables and footnotes.

  • Derived all counts from the embedded finding records at page load.
