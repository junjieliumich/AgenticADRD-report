Experiment summaries

Experiment E1 · source order

ComunPaz program

The article describes one experimental evaluation. Communities were assigned to a four-module program or comparison condition; the program targeted collaboration between state and communal authorities in former FARC strongholds.

  • 149 communities in 24 municipalities and four regions
  • 72 treatment communities
  • Implementation: October 2018–May 2019
  • Endline: November–December 2019, roughly seven months after intervention completion

Unresolved gaps and limitations

PDF-bounded coverage

The article repeatedly points to appendices and online supplementary materials for blocking details, balance tests, measurement details, multiple-comparison corrections, heterogeneity, and robustness checks. Those files are outside the supplied PDF, so their details remain outside this extraction.

Design limits stated by the authors

The four treatment modules cannot be separated causally; baseline data are unavailable for several moderators; low-level administrative crime data are absent; some outcomes have floor effects; and survey measures may face recall or social-desirability bias.

Inference and missingness

Three types of spillover could attenuate or complicate ITT estimates. Behavioral petition and WhatsApp outcomes are missing for 32 of 149 communities, though missingness was not statistically related to treatment or measured community controls.

Unresolved ambiguity

No finding is coded Pending. The PDF is readable, and its internal descriptions are sufficiently clear for the claims reported here. Details available only in the separate supplement are coded as coverage limits, not guessed.
