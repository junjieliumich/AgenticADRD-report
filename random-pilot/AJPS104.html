Class 1 · Primary field experiment: household clusters were randomly chosen as information seeds in each of two purposively selected villages, and respondent outcomes were measured after village-wide diffusion. The village ethnic composition was not randomized. The enumerator-order randomization is an implementation safeguard, not a separately analyzed experiment.
