Class 1 · A new sample was individually randomized among three civic-education video arms and a wildlife placebo, with formal OLS and Wald analyses. This is the article’s main causal study. It is labeled a survey experiment; gender stratification and unreported allocation probabilities do not support the stricter completely-randomized label.
