E1 · Five-arm parliamentary-debate exposure experiment (including two-day follow-up)

Class 1 · A new sample of 1,991 participants was individually assigned with equal probability to placebo, personal-background, policy, full-video, or full-audio exposure. The two-day survey reuses the original assignment and therefore belongs to this experiment. The segment and delivery-mode arms are not fully crossed, so the design is not labeled factorial.

Experiment design: completely randomized experiment; survey experiment
