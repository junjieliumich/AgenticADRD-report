EXP029-E1 · Budget-corruption SMS and treatment-density field experiment

Class 1 · Primary causal study. One experimental sample jointly received individual-level budget-information/placebo assignment, paired-village density assignment, and a concurrent fully crossed public-service-quality treatment. Because these manipulations are concurrent and fully crossed in the same sample, they are treated as one multi-level factorial experiment.

Experiment design: cluster-randomized experiment; factorial experiment
