Class 1 · This is the article's causal centerpiece: 40 Union Councils were randomized 20/20 to CBT plus enhanced routine care or enhanced routine care, with outcomes through seven years. The article does not establish whether assignment was unstratified, so 'completely randomized experiment' is not added as a separate label.
