E1 · Nationwide marriage-license responsiveness audit

Class 1 · The article's central 2×2 randomized email audit assigns requester and spouse gender at the jurisdiction/contact level and formally analyzes multiple response outcomes. Assignment used multivariate continuous blocking, so it is not labeled an unrestricted completely randomized experiment. All subgroup, outcome, weighting, and robustness analyses reuse this assignment; no separate random sample/manipulation is reported.
