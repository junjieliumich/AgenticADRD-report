EXP1 · INVEST vocational-training and cash-transfer factorial field experiment

Class 1 · Primary experiment: the article’s central causal analysis uses randomized TVET assignment. TVET and a one-time cash transfer were jointly randomized in four equal-probability cells, so this is one factorial experiment, not two separate experiments. The article’s course-duration, classroom-composition, and demographic analyses are nonrandomized subgroup analyses within EXP1, not new experiments.
