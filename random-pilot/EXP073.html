EXP073-E1 · Post-lockdown descriptive-norm × restaurant-certification field experiment

Class 1 · Primary randomized field experiment. Participants were individually blocked and independently assigned to descriptive-norm and certificate factors, creating a factorial design. The park procedure and moderator/measurement analyses use the same sample/assignment and are included here rather than counted as new experiments. The briefly named expanded prosocial-norm treatment is retained as an incompletely described attribute of this experiment because the main article does not establish a separate sample or assignment.
