Class 1 · A single cohort of 79 families was randomized among PC, PC+, and no-coaching control. PC and PC+ were later collapsed for the focal intervention-versus-control analyses. Repeated follow-ups and manual diagnostics reuse that original assignment and therefore belong to this experiment.
