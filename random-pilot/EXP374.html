EXP374-E1 · Haripur Lady Health Worker mission-motivation field experiment

Class 1 · Primary experiment. The 710-worker, clinic-blocked individual assignment covers status quo, placebo, financial incentive, mission, mission-plus, and nested mission-delivery variants. Endline and one-year outcomes reuse the original assignment and therefore belong to this experiment. The phone BDM-style elicitation did not create the reported Rs0 contrast through a new realized assignment (implementation was prevented), so it is treated as a follow-up outcome, not a separate randomized experiment.
