EXP058-E1 · Partisan-news homepage encouragement field experiment

Class 1 · A single sample of YouGov Pulse respondents was randomly assigned once in wave 3 to Fox News encouragement, HuffPost encouragement, or pure control. Study 1 and Study 2 are outcome families using that same assignment; later waves are follow-ups, not separate experiments. First-stage and robustness analyses also reuse the assignment.
