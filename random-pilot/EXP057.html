Class 1 · A new sample of 311 eligible Philadelphia County divorce seekers was individually randomized to a lawyer-matching effort versus alternative minimal assistance. ITT, CACE/LATE, balance, and sensitivity analyses all use this one original randomized assignment; no second randomized sample or manipulation appears.
