EXP406-E1 · Two-level saturation and village cash-transfer experiment

Class 1 · The article's central randomized evaluation. Saturation groups were randomized high/low, villages within groups were randomized to cash transfer/control, and rollout order was randomized. All household, enterprise, price, welfare, and multiplier follow-ups use this original assignment and therefore belong to this one experiment.
