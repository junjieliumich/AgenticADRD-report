E1 · Three-arm public-deliberation field experiment

Class 1 · This is the article's central randomized experiment. Participants were assigned individually to Public Deliberation, Individual Deliberation, or information-only Control; all outcome, moderation, and robustness analyses reuse that assignment. The assignment mechanism, probabilities, stratification, and restrictions are too incompletely reported to support the more specific 'completely randomized experiment' label, and the intervention is not merely embedded in a survey.
