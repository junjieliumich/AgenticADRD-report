EXP298-E1 · Moving to Opportunity randomized housing-voucher experiment and linked long-term follow-up

Class 1 · The article's central causal evidence uses the original MTO family-level random assignment. Child, adult, first-stage, subgroup, tax, and later follow-up analyses reuse that assignment and therefore belong to one experiment. The article reports no new participant randomization; simulated placebo reassignments are an inference procedure, not a separate experiment.
