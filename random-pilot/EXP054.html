EXP054-E1 · Municipality-level Solarize messaging field experiment

Class 1 · Primary experiment: the article's central causal study randomly assigns matched Connecticut municipalities to self-interest messaging, prosocial messaging, or control. Post-campaign adoption, adopter surveys, system characteristics, spatial clustering, and income moderation all reuse the original assignment and are follow-ups/analyses of this same experiment.

Exact treatment and comparator

All treated municipalities received roughly 20-week Solarize campaigns with a chosen installer, events, tours, local/social media, direct mail, signs, and volunteer interactions. Self-interest materials emphasized personal financial benefits; prosocial materials emphasized community benefits/social norms. The full message set and example leaflets are NR (deferred to SI). Control municipalities received no campaign during the experimental comparison.
