E1 · Precinct information-provision field experiment

Class 1 · Precincts, rather than individuals, were block-randomized to information; treated precincts also varied dissemination along two stated dimensions. All post-election follow-ups reuse this assignment and therefore belong to the same experiment.

Experiment design: cluster-randomized experiment; factorial experiment

Target population

Voters in predominantly rural electoral precincts in 17 municipalities in Guanajuato, México, San Luis Potosí, and Querétaro with usable Prospera-derived network data.

Eligibility criteria

Municipalities had 2015 local elections, passed safety/logistics protocols, and had a 2015 audit report; precinct selection prioritized accessible rural precincts and high/low malfeasance contexts. Exact safety/logistics and precinct eligibility thresholds are NR.

Recruitment procedure

Precincts were selected by the researchers; treated-survey respondents were randomly sampled leaflet recipients and control respondents were randomly sampled voters under a matched dissemination frame. Household approach and response/replacement procedures are NR.
