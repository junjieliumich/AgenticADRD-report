EXP229-E1 · Electoral rules and project-selection procedures in 250 Afghan villages

Class 1 · The article's principal causal evidence comes from one village-level field experiment. Electoral rule and project-selection procedure were assigned concurrently in four combinations to the same 250 villages; this is one factorial experiment, not two distinct randomized samples. Formal models and Tables 3–7 analyze the electoral-rule factor. Monitoring of a random subset measures implementation and is not a newly manipulated experiment.

Experiment design: cluster-randomized experiment; factorial experiment
