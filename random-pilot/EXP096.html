exp1 · Village-level PES randomized controlled trial with 2019 long-term follow-up

Class 1 · Primary causal experiment. Villages were randomized to a PES offer or control; all 2019 behavioral and survey outcomes reuse that original assignment and therefore belong to this experiment. The one-winner-per-village seedling lottery only incentivized outcome elicitation and created no analyzed treatment contrast, so it is not inventoried as a separate experiment.
