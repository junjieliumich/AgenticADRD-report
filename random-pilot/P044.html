Class 1 · Villages were randomized to BIMLI or an active-placebo English course; immediate student, four-month student, and parent outcomes all use the original village assignment and therefore form one primary experiment.

Experiment design: cluster-randomized experiment

Target population

School students in grades 8–12, ages 13–18, living in sampled villages around 100 Jeevika libraries across 32 districts of Bihar, India.

Eligibility criteria

Household appeared on Jeevika state-program lists; a child was in grades 8–12; household/child expressed interest and gave oral consent. Figure 1 mentions additional eligibility questions but does not report them.

Recruitment procedure

Jeevika supplied lists and staff visited eligible homes; 20–25 interested households per village were passed to the survey team for in-person baseline before randomization.
Jeevika staff visited these homes to ask whether the child would be interestedPDF page 8 · Sampling, Enrollment, and Baseline Data

Jeevika staff conducted household visits and generated lists of 20–25 interested households per village before the in-person baseline.

Field dates and setting

Recruitment June–September 2023; baseline September–November 2023; village randomization November 2023; after-school library classes November 2023–March 2024; first endline March–April 2024; phone follow-up August 2024.
from November 2023 to March 2024PDF page 7 · Administering Classes
