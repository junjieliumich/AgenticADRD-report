EXP060-E1 · Citizen monitoring in 120 Liberian forest communities

Class 1 · The article's central causal study randomizes 120 communities in a 2×2 design. Citizen monitoring is the focal factor; negotiation training is a cross-randomized second factor in the same sample and enters the single estimand/model. It is therefore one factorial experiment, not two independent experiments.

Experiment design: cluster-randomized experiment; factorial experiment
