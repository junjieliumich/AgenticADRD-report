Class 1 · One primary experiment: the same 176-village 8×2 assignment generated the direct, spillover, efficiency, threshold, education, and geodesic follow-up analyses. The within-village target selection is nested in this assignment rather than a new randomized sample. No separate supporting or diagnostic randomization, outcome-free pilot, or standalone observational study is reported in the main article.

Experiment design: cluster-randomized experiment; factorial experiment
