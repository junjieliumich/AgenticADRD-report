EXP408-E1 · Two-stage individualized consumer-choice price experiment

Class 1 · The article's sole causal experiment randomizes joint high/low product prices within participant across 18 choice trials. The earlier valuation stage calibrates this assignment and the later valuation is a follow-up outcome; randomized trial order, option location, and incentive lotteries are implementation devices rather than separately analyzed experiments.
