EXP113-E1 · Six-arm federal student-loan email field experiment

Class 1 · The article presents one new randomized sample with six borrower-level email arms and formal analyses. The four reminder arms cross percentage/dollar framing with repeated-two/sequential-one-action messaging, an embedded 2×2 factorial. All contrasts use the same randomized cohort and therefore remain one experiment.

Experiment design: completely randomized experiment; factorial experiment

Experimental arms or randomized attributes

Six arms: control email (255,300); two-action percentage-benefits without reminder (2,502,200); two-action percentage with reminder (2,501,500); two-action dollar with reminder (2,502,600); one-action percentage with reminder (2,502,600); one-action dollar with reminder (2,502,100), all rounded. Attributes are behaviorally informed versus control, reminder, percentage versus dollar framing, and repeated two-action versus sequential one-action messaging.
