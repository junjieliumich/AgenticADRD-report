P109-E1 · 2013 poverty-prime × mobility-prime survey experiment

Class 1 · This is the article's sole outcome-bearing random assignment and the primary test of Propositions 1 and 3. The two randomized primes are crossed in one 2×2 experiment; manipulation checks and subgroup/robustness analyses reuse that assignment and are not separate experiments. The article supports the factorial and survey-experiment labels but does not establish that treatment assignment was unstratified, so the stronger completely-randomized label is not applied.
