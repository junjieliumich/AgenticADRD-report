E1 · Five-task paired-profile armed-group governance conjoint

Class 1 · This is the article's central randomized study: 2,397 respondents each evaluated five pairs of hypothetical armed groups whose profile attributes were randomized. The same assignment supports forced-choice, rating, subgroup, and robustness analyses; those are not separate experiments.

Experiment design: factorial experiment; conjoint experiment; survey experiment
