EXP156-E1 · Bangladesh family preference-elicitation and associational study

Class 5 · The same family members completed incentivized time-, risk-, and social-preference elicitation tasks. Household selection was randomized for sampling, while task order and payoff realization were randomized as procedural features; none creates a reported treatment-control contrast. The main article does not define or estimate an outcome contrast by randomized order or payoff assignment. Its focal estimates are correlations, regressions of one family member's measured preference on another's, and family clustering. Accordingly, the study is observational for this review and is not scored as a randomized experiment.
