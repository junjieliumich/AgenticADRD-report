Experimental arms or randomized attributes

Unit of randomization

Individual workers were sorted within line/section × religion × skill pools and allocated to line-section teams; completed line-level teams were then randomly allocated to cohorts.

Allocation probabilities and restrictions

Two of three cohorts within a line received one line-team type and the third the other; direction depended on available Hindu/Muslim counts. Mixed sections targeted 35%–40% Muslim; section switching was minimized; assignment was stratified by section, religion, and skill. Exact individual assignment probabilities are NR.
