E1 · Birmingham partisan telephone campaign and household-spillover experiment

Class 1 · This is the article's sole newly analyzed randomized assignment. One person per two-voter household was blocked by party preference and assigned to high-intensity call, low-intensity call, or no-contact control. Direct ITT, household-spillover, conditional logistic, and CACE analyses all reuse this assignment and are therefore part of one experiment.

Experiment design: completely randomized experiment
