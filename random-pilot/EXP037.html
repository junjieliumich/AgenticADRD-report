EXP037-E1 · Phase III stove supply-and-promotion field experiment

Class 1 · This is the article's principal randomized test. The intervention bundle was assigned at hamlet level, with rebate level randomized among households in treated hamlets. The nested rebate randomization is part of the same intervention and sample, not a new randomized sample.

Target population

Rural households in selected gram panchayats of Bageshwar and Nainital districts, Uttarakhand, India; the article cautions that the sample may not represent all rural Uttarakhand.
sampling framePDF page 6 · Materials and Methods—Sample Design

The experimental frame was 97 geographically distinct hamlets in 38 gram panchayats in Bageshwar and Nainital districts of Uttarakhand.

may not be representativePDF page 6 · Materials and Methods—Sample Design

The methods explicitly caution that the sample may not represent all rural Uttarakhand, while noting that it spans NGO-work and matched comparison gram panchayats.

Eligibility criteria

A sampled household needed the primary cook and/or head of household available as an eligible respondent.
eligible respondentPDF page 6 · Materials and Methods—Sample Design

Eligibility required the primary cook and/or household head to be available; refusals and whole-day unavailability led to neighbor replacement.

Recruitment procedure

GPs were selected from a census using NGO-presence plus propensity-score matching; households were systematically sampled (every nth household) with randomly selected neighboring replacements.
final sample at baselinePDF page 6 · Materials and Methods—Sample Design

The baseline sample was 1,063 households, selected systematically within 38 sampled gram panchayats; unavailable, ineligible, or refusing households were replaced by randomly selected neighbors.

Field dates and setting

Baseline summer 2012; intervention summer 2013; first follow-up about 3 months later; second follow-up about 15 months later; rural Uttarakhand in the Indian Himalayas.
surveys were repeatedPDF page 6 · Materials and Methods—Survey Design and Implementation

Surveys occurred at baseline, during the intervention, and at two follow-ups; the first follow-up was about 3 months and the second about 15 months after intervention.
