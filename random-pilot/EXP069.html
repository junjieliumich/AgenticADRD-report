EXP069-E1 · Twitter bot copartisanship and identification-strength field experiment

Class 1 · This is the article's principal causal study: 842 Twitter users were assigned within homogeneous blocks to bot partisanship concordance and identification strength. Both randomized factors are crossed, supporting the factorial-experiment label. Blocking rules out the prompt's completely randomized label; assignment is at the user, not cluster, level, and the design is not conjoint, survey, or list.
