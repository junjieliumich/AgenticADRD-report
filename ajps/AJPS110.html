Class 1 · Primary causal study: five email attributes were randomized across the 408-office census and formally analyzed with OLS/AMCE models. The 24-office pretest is retained as a separately inventoried pilot phase because its Turkish female alias and field date differ, although the article pools it into the primary analysis.
