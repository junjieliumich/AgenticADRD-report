Class 1 · Primary experiment. One common four-factor vignette was randomized once per respondent across five regional samples, with country-tailored authority labels and pooled focal analyses. The sites are treated as one multi-site experiment because the article reports one protocol and one joint statistical analysis rather than independent country experiments.
