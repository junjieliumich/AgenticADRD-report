E1 · Six-case belief-correction survey experiment

Class 1 · Primary experiment: the July 2021 online China survey randomly assigned respondents to learn versus not learn the actual police-detention outcomes for six cases and formally analyzed post-treatment attitudes and policy preferences. Later diagnostic and robustness outcomes use this original assignment.
