Class 1 · Primary experiment: the article's central causal test randomly assigns individual PAPI respondents to EVFTA economic-risk exposure and analyzes subsequent information-seeking. Manipulation checks, placebo-site outcomes, subgroup analyses, and RI reuse this assignment and are part of the same experiment.
