AJPS146-E1 · Three-task hypothetical-candidate policy conjoint experiment

Class 1 · Primary experiment. This is the article's central randomized conjoint: the same 6,070-person survey generates three randomized candidate-choice tasks per respondent. The respondent-specific issue draw, profile-level draws, and response-option polarity are design randomizations within this one experiment, not new randomized samples or separately analyzed experiments. No embedded list experiment was found.
