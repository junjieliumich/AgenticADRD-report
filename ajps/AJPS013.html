E1 · Barangay-level randomized deliberative campaign experiment

Class 1 · Primary experiment: 39 barangays were assigned within 13 municipalities to a town-hall deliberative campaign or business-as-usual campaigning. Official-election outcomes and the two-week post-election survey are analyses/follow-up measurements of the same original assignment, not separate experiments.
